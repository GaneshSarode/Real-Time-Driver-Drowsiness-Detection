Powered by Research

Aegis Drive implements geometric facial landmark algorithms inspired by Hassan et al., published in Scientific Reports 2025 by Nature Publishing Group.

The system uses MediaPipe Face Mesh with Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) and Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR) computations for real-time drowsiness classification. All processing runs entirely on-device using WebAssembly and GPU acceleration.
